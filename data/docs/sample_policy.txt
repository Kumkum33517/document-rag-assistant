Campus Library Policy

Borrowing: Each student can borrow up to 4 books at a time for a period of 14 days.
Renewals: A book can be renewed twice, provided no other student has reserved it.
Fines: A late return is fined Rs 5 per day per book.
Lost books: If a book is lost, the student must pay the replacement cost of the book plus a Rs 200 processing fee.
Timings: The reading room is open from 8 AM to 10 PM on weekdays and 9 AM to 5 PM on Saturdays. The library is closed on Sundays.
Alumni: Alumni can get library membership for Rs 1000 per year and may borrow up to 2 books at a time.
